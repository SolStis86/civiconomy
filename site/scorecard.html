Scorecard

Make ownership visible.

The ownership accountability scorecard will help people understand how businesses are owned, financed, governed, and operated.

Draft categories

Ownership

Who owns the business and who has control rights?

Workers

How are workers paid, represented, informed, and included?

Capital

How much debt is used and where does value flow?

Tax and transparency

Does the company publish enough for accountability?

Reinvestment

Does profit strengthen the operating business?

Community

Does the company support or weaken the places it depends on?
