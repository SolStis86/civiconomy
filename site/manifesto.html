Manifesto

Ownership is power.

Civiconomy begins with a simple belief: the economy should strengthen the people, places, and systems that make enterprise possible.

The economy should belong to the people who build it.

Capital has a role. Profit has a role. Investment has a role. But none of them should sit above human dignity, productive work, community resilience, or democratic accountability.

Civiconomy exists to build practical alternatives: better ownership models, better transition pathways, better capital structures, and better public understanding of how enterprise is governed.

We are not trying to decorate the existing economy with softer language. We are trying to make better ownership investable, understandable, replicable, and visible.

What we affirm

  • Workers are co-creators of value.
  • Founders deserve fair exits that protect legacy.
  • Communities deserve stable productive assets.
  • Investors deserve fair returns, not unchecked supremacy.
  • Capital should serve civilisation.

What we build

Democratic capital

Capital aligned with long-term productive value and shared benefit.

Ownership pathways

Routes from concentrated ownership toward worker, steward, community, and hybrid models.

Public accountability

Transparent ways to assess ownership quality and enterprise conduct.
