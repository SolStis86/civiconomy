Manifesto

Ownership is not just finance. Ownership is power.

Civiconomy exists because the ownership of enterprise shapes the lives of workers, the strength of communities, the resilience of firms, and the direction of society.

The economy should belong to the people who build it.

Capital has a role. Profit has a role. Investment has a role. But none of them should sit above human dignity, productive work, community resilience, or democratic accountability.

Civiconomy is a blueprint for better ownership: ownership that is patient, accountable, participatory, productive, and visible.

What we believe

  • Capital should serve civilisation.
  • Workers are co-creators of value.
  • Founders deserve succession routes that protect legacy as well as price.
  • Communities deserve productive businesses that remain resilient and accountable.
  • Investors deserve fair returns, but not supremacy over every other stakeholder.
  • Profit is strongest when it comes from productivity, service, innovation, and stewardship.

What we are building

Democratic capital

Capital aligned with long-term productive value, fair returns, worker participation, and community benefit.

Ownership pathways

Routes toward employee ownership, steward ownership, cooperative models, community ownership, and hybrid structures.

Public accountability

Scorecards and explainers that make ownership quality visible to workers, founders, citizens, customers, and policymakers.

The invitation

If you are a founder, worker, investor, researcher, designer, developer, policymaker, or citizen who believes ownership can be better designed, Civiconomy is an invitation to build.
