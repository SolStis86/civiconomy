Founders

A better succession route for good businesses.

Civiconomy can become a credible route for founders who want fair value, continuity, and long-term protection for the company they built.

Founder pathway

Fair exit

A founder should be paid fairly for what they built.

Legacy protection

The company purpose, culture, people, and customers should be protected through transition.

Transition finance

Capital should help the company move into a stronger long-term ownership model.

First checklist

  • What does the founder want protected?
  • What ownership structure is realistic?
  • What role should workers have?
  • What finance is required?
  • What would success look like five years after transition?
