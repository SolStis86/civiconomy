People-powered economics for the age of concentrated ownership.

Build the economy democracy deserves.

Civiconomy is a blueprint for replacing extractive ownership with democratic capital, accountable enterprise, and shared prosperity.

It starts with a simple idea: the economy should belong to the people who build it.

Read the manifesto See the scorecard concept

The problem

Too much of the modern economy is governed by ownership models that reward extraction, short-term pressure, and concentrated control.

Ownership decides outcomes

Who owns a business shapes wages, investment, debt, resilience, culture, and who benefits from success.

Good firms need better exits

Founders often need succession routes that protect workers, customers, community value, and legacy.

The public needs visibility

People should be able to see whether a company is governed for stewardship, extraction, resilience, or short-term gain.

Private equity concentrates ownership. Democratic capital distributes ownership.

What Civiconomy builds

Manifesto

A clear public case for why ownership must become a civic question.

Blueprint

A practical architecture for capital, governance, transition pathways, and public accountability.

Scorecard

A simple model for assessing ownership quality, worker voice, debt burden, transparency, and reinvestment.

Who it is for

Founders

For owners who want fair succession without losing the soul of the company.

Workers

For people who believe contribution should create voice, stake, and shared upside.

Communities

For places that need productive businesses to remain rooted, resilient, and accountable.

Investors

Builders
