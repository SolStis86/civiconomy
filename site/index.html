The economy should belong to the people who build it.

Civiconomy is a blueprint for moving from extractive ownership toward a people-powered economy: one where capital serves enterprise, workers share in value, founders preserve legacy, and communities benefit from durable stewardship.

What Civiconomy is building

A public manifesto

A clear moral and practical case for making ownership more democratic, accountable, and productive.

An ownership scorecard

A way to make ownership quality visible through worker voice, debt burden, tax conduct, reinvestment, and community impact.

Transition pathways

Practical routes for founders, workers, investors, and communities to move businesses into better ownership models.

Private equity concentrates ownership. Democratic capital distributes ownership.

The first mission

Create a practical alternative for good businesses that need succession, investment, stability, or transition without losing their purpose.

For founders

Protect legacy while creating fair exit routes.

For workers

Turn contribution into voice, stake, and shared upside.

For communities

Keep productive assets rooted, resilient, and accountable.
