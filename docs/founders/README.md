# Founder Pathway

The Civiconomy founder pathway is for owners who want a fair exit while protecting the long-term health of the business they built.

## Purpose

Many good businesses reach a transition point when a founder wants to retire, step back, or reduce risk. The usual options can be narrow: sell to a financial buyer, sell to a competitor, pass to family, appoint management, or close.

Civiconomy should provide another route: a structured transition into better long-term ownership.

## Core Promise

A founder should not have to choose between fair value and the future of the company.

## Pathway Goals

- Protect founder legacy.
- Preserve jobs and operating knowledge.
- Give workers a meaningful future stake.
- Keep customer and supplier relationships stable.
- Avoid unnecessary short-term pressure.
- Move the company toward a durable ownership structure.

## Possible Outcomes

- Employee ownership.
- Steward ownership.
- Community ownership for local assets.
- Long-term civic holding company ownership.
- Hybrid models combining founder, worker, investor, and steward interests.

## First Conversation

The first conversation should establish:

1. What the founder wants to protect.
2. What the business needs to remain strong.
3. What workers should gain from transition.
4. What level of capital is required.
5. Which ownership structure fits the company.
