# Founder Succession Checklist

Use this checklist to assess whether a business may be suitable for a Civiconomy-style transition.

## 1. Founder Intent

- Does the founder want to retire, step back, or reduce risk?
- Does the founder care about legacy and continuity?
- Is the founder open to a structured transition period?
- Is fair value more important than maximum short-term price?

## 2. Business Strength

- Is the business profitable or capable of stable recovery?
- Does it provide useful goods, services, employment, or local capacity?
- Does it have loyal customers or strong supplier relationships?
- Does it depend heavily on the founder personally?

## 3. Worker Readiness

- Do workers have the skills and commitment needed for continuity?
- Is there a capable management layer?
- Are workers likely to support an ownership transition?
- Are there existing consultation or representation structures?

## 4. Financial Suitability

- What is the realistic valuation?
- What debt does the business already carry?
- What transition capital would be required?
- Can the business support a gradual ownership transfer?

## 5. Ownership Fit

- Would employee ownership suit the company?
- Would steward ownership protect its purpose?
- Would community ownership matter for local assets?
- Would a hybrid model be more practical?

## 6. Risk Review

- Are there hidden liabilities?
- Are margins too weak for transition finance?
- Is the business overly dependent on one customer, supplier, or person?
- Are there governance gaps that need repair first?

## Initial Outcome

Classify the opportunity as:

- Strong candidate
- Possible candidate with work required
- Not currently suitable
- Needs more information
