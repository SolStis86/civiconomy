# Scorecard Categories

## 1. Ownership Structure

Assesses who owns the business, who has control rights, and whether ownership is aligned with long-term enterprise health.

### Evidence Needed

- Ownership records
- Shareholder or parent company structure
- Voting rights
- Governance rights
- Exit or sale history

### Positive Indicators

- Clear ownership structure
- Long-term stewardship intent
- Worker, community, founder, or mission-aligned stake
- Limits on short-term extraction

### Warning Signs

- Opaque ownership
- Complex offshore structures without clear purpose
- Frequent resale
- Control rights separated from operating responsibility

## 2. Worker Voice

Assesses whether workers have meaningful routes to information, participation, and representation.

### Evidence Needed

- Worker councils or forums
- Union recognition or equivalent participation
- Board representation
- Consultation process
- Information-sharing practices

### Positive Indicators

- Regular structured consultation
- Worker representation in governance
- Clear escalation routes
- Transparent communication

### Warning Signs

- No worker participation structure
- Poor communication
- No formal consultation routes
- Retaliation concerns or fear culture

## 3. Worker Reward

Assesses whether workers share fairly in enterprise success.

### Evidence Needed

- Pay data
- Bonus and profit-sharing policy
- Benefits
- Ownership participation
- Median pay trend

### Positive Indicators

- Profit sharing
- Employee ownership participation
- Strong benefits
- Real wage progression
- Sensible executive-to-worker pay ratio

### Warning Signs

- Low pay despite strong profits
- Very high executive-to-worker pay gap
- Insecure contracts
- Little or no upside sharing

## 4. Debt and Capital Structure

Assesses whether debt and capital are used to strengthen or weaken the operating business.

### Evidence Needed

- Debt levels
- Interest costs
- Dividend history
- Acquisition finance structure
- Credit rating or lender notes where available

### Positive Indicators

- Debt used for productive investment
- Sustainable interest cover
- Clear reinvestment strategy
- No excessive distributions during stress

### Warning Signs

- Heavy debt relative to cash flow
- Debt used mainly to fund distributions
- Rising interest pressure
- Reduced resilience after acquisition

## 5. Reinvestment

Assesses whether profits are reinvested into the business, workers, customers, and long-term resilience.

### Evidence Needed

- Capital expenditure
- Training spend
- R&D spend
- Maintenance spend
- Technology and process improvement

### Positive Indicators

- Consistent reinvestment
- Training and skills growth
- Maintenance of core assets
- Investment in quality and resilience

### Warning Signs

- Underinvestment
- Deferred maintenance
- Cost cutting that weakens service
- Profit extraction ahead of business health

## 6. Tax Conduct

Assesses whether the company contributes responsibly to the public systems it depends on.

### Evidence Needed

- Tax strategy
- Effective tax rate
- Jurisdictional structure
- Public tax reporting
- Disputes or settlements

### Positive Indicators

- Clear tax strategy
- Responsible effective tax rate
- Transparent reporting
- No unnecessary complexity

### Warning Signs

- Aggressive tax planning
- Low tax rate without clear reason
- Profit shifting indicators
- Secrecy-heavy structure

## 7. Transparency

Assesses whether stakeholders can understand the company’s ownership, finances, governance, and impact.

### Evidence Needed

- Annual reports
- Ownership disclosures
- Governance disclosures
- Worker and impact reporting
- Public policies

### Positive Indicators

- Clear public reporting
- Accessible plain-English summaries
- Transparent ownership and governance
- Published impact data

### Warning Signs

- Minimal disclosure
- Hard-to-trace ownership
- Missing governance information
- Inconsistent reporting

## 8. Community Impact

Assesses whether the business strengthens or weakens the places and systems it depends on.

### Evidence Needed

- Local employment data
- Supplier practices
- Service continuity
- Community investment
- Environmental and social impact data

### Positive Indicators

- Stable local employment
- Local supplier support
- Community reinvestment
- Responsible environmental conduct

### Warning Signs

- Sudden site closures without transition support
- Local value leakage
- Poor supplier treatment
- Unaddressed local harm
