# Example Company Profile

This file demonstrates the intended structure for a Civiconomy scorecard profile. It uses a fictional company.

## Company

**ExampleCo Ltd**

## Summary

ExampleCo is a mid-sized operating business with stable revenue, a visible ownership structure, and early worker participation practices. It has no employee ownership model yet, but has scope for transition.

## Overall Grade

**B- / Credible but improvable**

## Category Scores

| Category | Score | Confidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Ownership structure | 3 / 5 | Medium |
| Worker voice | 2 / 5 | Medium |
| Worker reward | 3 / 5 | Low |
| Debt and capital structure | 4 / 5 | Medium |
| Reinvestment | 3 / 5 | Medium |
| Tax conduct | 3 / 5 | Low |
| Transparency | 3 / 5 | Medium |
| Community impact | 4 / 5 | Low |

## Evidence Notes

- Ownership appears straightforward.
- Debt levels appear manageable.
- Worker participation exists but is informal.
- Public reporting is limited.
- No clear profit-sharing mechanism is visible.

## Positive Indicators

- Stable operating business.
- Clear local employment role.
- No obvious excessive debt pressure.
- Potential for founder succession planning.

## Warning Signs

- Limited worker voice.
- Limited transparency on pay and tax.
- No published ownership transition plan.

## Recommendations

1. Publish a plain-English ownership and governance summary.
2. Create a worker consultation structure.
3. Explore profit sharing or employee ownership participation.
4. Publish basic reinvestment and community impact indicators.
5. Assess suitability for a long-term stewardship or employee ownership pathway.

## Confidence

This profile is based on limited public information. Scores should be treated as provisional until supported by verified data.
