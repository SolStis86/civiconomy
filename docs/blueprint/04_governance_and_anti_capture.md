# Governance and Anti-Capture

## Design Aim

Civiconomy should be governed so that no single group can dominate the mission, capital allocation process, or portfolio strategy.

## Three-House Model

### Investor House

Protects capital discipline, risk management, solvency, and fiduciary credibility.

### Worker and Community House

Protects worker voice, community outcomes, democratic ownership, and social legitimacy.

### Stewardship House

Acts as the independent guardian of the founding mission, transparency standards, conflicts policy, and long-term public trust.

## Consent Principle

Major decisions should require cross-house consent. This prevents the organisation becoming either financially reckless or missionless.

## Safeguards

- Published conflicts register.
- Independent audit.
- Transparent remuneration policy.
- Portfolio-level reporting.
- Whistleblower channel.
- Rotating representative seats.
- Limits on insider benefit.
- Clear separation between investment decisions and public campaigning.

## Governance Test

A governance design is acceptable only if it can survive growth, criticism, financial pressure, and attempts at capture.
