# Accountability Scorecard

The accountability scorecard makes ownership visible.

## Purpose

The scorecard should help citizens, workers, founders, investors, journalists, customers, and policymakers understand whether a business is governed for stewardship or short-term extraction.

## Core Categories

1. Ownership structure.
2. Debt burden.
3. Worker pay and security.
4. Worker voice.
5. Executive-to-worker pay ratio.
6. Profit distribution.
7. Tax conduct.
8. Reinvestment levels.
9. Community impact.
10. Environmental responsibility.
11. Political influence and lobbying transparency.
12. Progress toward democratic ownership.

## Output

Each business should receive:

- a summary grade,
- category scores,
- plain-English explanation,
- data sources,
- improvement recommendations,
- ownership transition potential.

## Principle

The portfolio is not only a collection of assets. It is an argument. Every business should help prove that better ownership is possible.
