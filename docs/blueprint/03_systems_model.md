# Systems Model

Civiconomy is designed as a system of institutions, tools, and practices that can support democratic enterprise at multiple levels.

## Institution Layer

- Democratic capital funds.
- Civic holding companies.
- Public-interest credit facilities.
- Ownership transition advisory services.
- Long-term stewardship trusts.

## Enterprise Layer

- Worker participation structures.
- Profit sharing.
- Transparent operating dashboards.
- Ownership transition plans.
- Founder legacy protection.
- Community impact reporting.

## Infrastructure Layer

- Legal templates.
- Due diligence models.
- Public scorecards.
- Educational materials.
- Founder outreach materials.
- Worker education materials.

## Movement Layer

- A public narrative.
- A shared glossary.
- Local chapters or partner organisations.
- Sector research.
- Public case studies.

The goal is to make better ownership models easier to understand, finance, govern, and replicate.
