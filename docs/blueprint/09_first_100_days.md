# First 100 Days

## Days 1-10: Define the Spine

- Finalise mission, ideology, operating doctrine, and glossary.
- Create a public README and blueprint index.
- Identify the first advisory contributors.

## Days 11-25: Build the Public Frame

- Launch a simple public site.
- Publish the founding manifesto.
- Create explainers for workers, founders, investors, and citizens.
- Prepare visual messaging and social assets.

## Days 26-45: Build the Research Base

- Choose priority sectors.
- Research ownership harms and alternative models.
- Draft the accountability scorecard.
- Identify examples of successful worker, cooperative, steward, and community ownership.

## Days 46-65: Design the Institution

- Map legal structures.
- Draft fund architecture.
- Design governance and anti-capture mechanisms.
- Prepare pilot investment criteria.

## Days 66-85: Recruit and Validate

- Contact advisors, founders, unions, councils, ethical investors, and legal experts.
- Validate the founder succession thesis.
- Identify pilot candidates.

## Days 86-100: Publish and Mobilise

- Publish the full blueprint.
- Release the scorecard prototype.
- Publish a pilot roadmap.
- Open a contributor pipeline.
- Prepare the first campaign around better ownership.
