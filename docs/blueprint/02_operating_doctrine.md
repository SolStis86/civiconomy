# Operating Doctrine

## Mission

Civiconomy exists to acquire, finance, protect, and improve productive enterprises so ownership, power, and upside can progressively shift toward workers, communities, founders, and accountable long-term stewards.

## Five Core Functions

1. Protect good businesses from succession failure, avoidable collapse, or short-term financial pressure.
2. Convert ownership over time through employee ownership, steward ownership, cooperative models, community ownership, capped-return structures, or public-interest holding companies.
3. Share upside fairly between workers, founders, investors, customers, communities, and the wider system.
4. Build operational excellence through better systems, technology, governance, training, procurement, strategy, and resilience.
5. Create visible proof that democratic capital can produce durable long-term value.

## Non-Negotiable Principles

- Responsible use of debt.
- No unnecessary weakening of operating businesses.
- No business model based on low worker security.
- Clear and responsible tax conduct.
- Independence from party-political control.
- Transparency by default.
- Workers must gain meaningful participation, not just benefits.
- Founders should be honoured, not erased.
- Investors deserve fairness, not supremacy.
- The movement must include anti-corruption safeguards by design.
