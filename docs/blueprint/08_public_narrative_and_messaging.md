# Public Narrative and Messaging

## Core Message

The economy should belong to the people who build it.

## Strategic Contrast

Private equity concentrates ownership. Democratic capital distributes ownership.

Private equity often seeks short-term returns. Civiconomy seeks durable public value, fair returns, worker participation, and resilient enterprise.

## Audience Messages

### Workers

You should have a voice, a stake, and a share in the value you help create.

### Founders

Your business can outlive your ownership without losing its soul.

### Investors

Long-term value can be built without undermining the people and systems that make enterprise possible.

### Communities

Local businesses, essential services, and productive assets can be protected through better ownership.

### Policymakers

A healthier economy requires ownership models that reward stewardship, productivity, and resilience.

## Slogan Bank

- Ownership without extraction.
- Capital in service of civilisation.
- The economy should belong to the people who build it.
- Democratic capital compounds value.
- Better ownership is possible.
- Make stewardship investable.
- Make extraction visible.
- Build the economy democracy deserves.
