# Transition Pathways

Civiconomy needs practical routes for moving real businesses into better ownership models.

## Founder Succession

Many founders want to retire without seeing their companies weakened or broken up. Civiconomy can offer a credible exit route that protects legacy, workers, customers, and community value.

## Employee Ownership

Support conversion into Employee Ownership Trusts, direct employee share plans, cooperative structures, or hybrid participation models.

## Steward Ownership

Use trust or foundation-like structures to lock purpose, protect mission, and limit purely extractive control rights.

## Community Ownership

For local assets and essential services, community ownership can protect continuity, accountability, and local benefit.

## Patient Capital Buyout

Use long-term capital to acquire and improve productive businesses without forcing a short resale timeline.

## Rescue and Stabilisation

Where useful businesses face distress, Civiconomy can provide stabilisation capital, operating support, and a path toward democratic ownership.

## Transition Sequence

1. Identify suitable businesses.
2. Assess value, risk, ownership, workers, debt, and social importance.
3. Build a transition finance plan.
4. Agree governance and worker participation.
5. Improve operations.
6. Publish progress.
7. Move ownership gradually toward the chosen long-term model.
